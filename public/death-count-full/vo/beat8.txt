"Go talk to her," Sam says. So you go to the diner. The same booth. Dusk through the window. Two coffees. Still untouched. Your sister is there. Her timer says 14. You sit down. You speak. First words in three years. "You came." "I missed you." The number doesn't change. Fourteen is still fourteen. But you stop counting. You ask her about her day. She laughs. You forgot what that sounds like. You stay for an hour. Then two. She tells you about school. About her friends. About the book she's reading. You tell her about the numbers. She doesn't laugh. She just listens. For the first time in three years, the table doesn't feel like a wall. You can't add days to her number. But you can fill the days in it. The timer is still there. But you're not looking at it anymore. You're looking at her. Connections give our days meaning. The days don't matter. The people do. The next morning, you sit with Sam at lunch. And Maya. You don't check anyone's number. Outside, the city glows with numbers. Thousands of them. All at once. You don't count them anymore. You just walk home. With your sister. She talks the whole way. You let her. Fourteen days. You plan to use every one of them. So... what would yours say? Tell us in the comments.

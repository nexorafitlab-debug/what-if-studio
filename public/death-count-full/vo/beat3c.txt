The numbers are real. They move. You start doing small kindnesses. For the small numbers. You carry Mr. Ortiz's boxes. You hold doors. You give up seats. It feels good. Like you finally know what to do with it. You check the whole cafeteria. Hundreds of numbers. All at once. Most are big. Thousands of days. Years and years. A few are small. Too small. You look away. And then you see the pattern. The numbers are brightest over the people you already know. The strangers are dim. Like a light turned low. The people you love burn bright. You feel like you have a superpower. You can finally see who matters. You just don't know what "matters" costs yet.

You check the whole cafeteria. Hundreds of numbers. All at once. Most are big. Thousands of days. Years and years. A few are small. Too small. You look away. And then you see the pattern. The numbers are brightest over the people you already know. The strangers are dim. Like a light turned low. The people you love burn bright. You feel like you have a superpower. You can finally see who matters. You just don't know what "matters" costs yet.

That night, you look in the mirror. Nothing above your head. You turn. You stand on a chair. You hold up your phone. Nothing. You ask Sam what he sees. "Nothing," he says. "Just you." You try everything. The bathroom mirror. The window. A shop window. A puddle. A photo. Nothing. You flip through old photos. Every head is blank. Even yours. Even then. Every photo. Every year. Every single one. You climb to the roof at dusk. The city glows with numbers. Yours is blank. The one number you actually want, the only one that matters, you'll never get. You can't see your own.

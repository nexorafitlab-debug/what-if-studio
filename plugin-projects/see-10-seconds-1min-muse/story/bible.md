# What If You Could See 10 Seconds Ahead? — 1-minute sample bible

Hero: **Kian, 17**. Black round head, blue hoodie #2e6ff0. Warm rim light on one side of the head.
Rule: he sees exactly 10 seconds ahead, only his own future, never off.
Voice: Helio (avocado_v2:MAI_03), 129 spoken words, 57.73s.

## Formula
Cold open (fan blade) → named hero + rule → three quick proofs (tray, pencil, bus) → the choice (rooftop phone) → FREEZE → the girl catches it → black-screen hook: "What happens when you stop preventing... and start choosing?"

## Sets (7, one per beat, all drawn fresh)
1. bedroom_dawn — dawn light, ceiling fan (the star prop)
2. hallway — school hallway, lockers, bulletin board
3. cafeteria — serving counter, long tables, hanging lamps
4. examhall — rows of desks, blackboard, wall clock
5. street_bus — dusk street, bus at the stop, glowing open door
6. rooftop — sunset, skyline, railing, AC unit, water tower
7. void — black screen + faint stars (title + hook)

## Devices
- Countdown numbers as BIG gold on-screen numbers (10-9-8, 5-4-3, 2-1), ticking with the spoken numbers.
- Gold ALL-CAPS text for names, rules and the hook. No subtitles ever.
- FREEZE beat on the rooftop choice (s018): time stops, phone mid-air, girl reaching, "FREEZE" label.
- Walk cycles for hallway + street entries; one pose swap per cut; face swaps (surprised, calm, smile); blink + breathe so nobody stands dead still.
- Letterbox 40px (bars "wide"), 1280x720, 24fps.

## Audio

- SFX for every action (fan wobble, blade catch, tray, pencil snap, bus brakes + door hiss, phone slip, wind, freeze).

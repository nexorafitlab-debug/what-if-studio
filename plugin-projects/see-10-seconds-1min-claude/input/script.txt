WHAT IF YOU COULD SEE 10 SECONDS AHEAD? — 1-MINUTE SAMPLE SCRIPT
Written by Claude Code, 2026-11-22. Hero: Kian, 17.
Rule: he sees exactly 10 seconds ahead, only his own future, never off.
Spoken words: ~130 (incl. countdown numbers). Target: ~1 minute.

[BEDROOM - DAWN]
You wake up. The ceiling fan spins. Three seconds later — it wobbles. [10... 9... 8...] You catch the falling blade before it hits your pillow.

[SCHOOL HALLWAY]
Kian. That's your name. Seventeen. You see everything ten seconds before it happens. Exactly ten. Only your own future. Never off.

[CAFETERIA]
Lunch tray slips. [5... 4... 3...] Your hand snags it mid-air. No spill. No stares.

[EXAM HALL]
Pencil snaps. [2... 1...] Spare appears in your grip. Teacher never notices.

[BUSY STREET]
Bus brakes. Doors hiss. You're already stepping on. The crowd surges behind you — three seconds late.

[ROOFTOP - SUNSET]
Wind gusts. Your phone slips. [10... 9... 8...] You watch it fall. You *could* catch it. But the vision shows the girl behind you — she reaches. She catches it. She smiles.

[BLACK SCREEN]
What happens when you stop preventing... and start choosing?

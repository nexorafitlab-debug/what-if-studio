# What If Every Lie You Tell Grows A Feather? — 1-min sample bible

## Hero
Kai, 16. Black round head, gold rim light, BURGUNDY hoodie **#800020** (locked),
dark pants, white sneakers. Voice: Helio (avocado_v2:MAI_03).

## Feather device (scenes/_feathers.js)
- drawFeather: central shaft, tapered slightly-curved vane, fine barb lines.
  White: fill #f5f2e8, shade #d8d2c0. Black: fill #16161d, shade #2e2e3a.
- featherPop: scale 0 -> 1 with ease-out-back over ~0.3 s + a tiny white ring burst.
- featherFall: drifts down with sinusoidal sway + slow rotation + fade near the
  end, like snow; black -> white lerp over the first 30% of the fall when
  turnWhite is set.
- featherCount: lunch count tile (dark tile, big gold number word, labels below)
  — built and stills-tested here; used in the FULL video's lunch beat.
- Anchors on the figure: wrist (front hand), collar (just below the head),
  chest (over the heart), shoulder. Feathers sit ON the body, never behind it,
  never tiny: wrist close-ups use len 55-115, collar 62-128, rule cards 95-125.
- Only Kai's own feathers are ever shown. A feather falls only when the truth
  is said out loud; it turns white and floats away like snow.

## The rule (stated in the sample)
Small lie = small white feather. Big lie = big black feather. It grows in one
second, right where everyone can see it. You cannot pull it out — it hurts and
grows back bigger. The only way to lose a feather is to tell the truth, out
loud — then it falls and floats away. You cannot see anyone else's. Only yours.

## Sample beats (152 words, 55.18 s)
Kitchen cold open (Mom: "Did you do your homework?" — Kai lies "Yes." — a white
feather POPS on his wrist, grows like a sprout, sleeve pulled down fast) ->
name card (KAI, 16) -> the rule (small white / big black, one second, everyone
can see it, cannot pull it out, grows back bigger, "Can you stop it?") ->
the way out (tell the truth — the feather falls and floats away) -> only yours
(classroom, no feathers on anyone else) -> "That is the rule." -> title card
(slow push-in on Kai).

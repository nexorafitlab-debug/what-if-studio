Monday morning. Your mom stands in the kitchen. Did you do your homework? You did not. You played games till midnight. You say "Yes." And something moves on your wrist... A feather. Small. White. Pop. It pushes out of your skin like a sprout. You pull your sleeve down. Fast. Your name is Kai. You are sixteen. And every lie you tell grows a feather. A small lie grows a small white feather. A big lie grows a big black one. It grows in one second... Right where everyone can see it. You cannot pull it out. It hurts... And it grows back. Bigger. Can you stop it? The only way to lose a feather is to tell the truth. Say it out loud... And the feather falls. It floats away. You cannot see anyone else's. Only yours. That is the rule. So what if every lie you tell grows a feather?

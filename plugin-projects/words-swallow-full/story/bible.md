## Swallowed-words device
Grey ALL-CAPS text (#b8b8c0, soft glow) floats just above a person's head, hangs,
then fades — once a word fades it is gone forever. Long lines split into two.
When someone finally SAYS a swallowed word out loud, the grey text turns GOLD
(#ffd76a) and rises like light (goldWord) — exactly three times: the girl's
apology (schoolyard), Grandma's words (Room 312), Dad's "DAD, I FORGIVE YOU"
(bedroom). Eli's OWN words are visible only in a mirror — never directly above
his head (mirrorWords).

## The rule (stated once, early)
You can see the words. You can NEVER make anyone say them. Once a word fades,
it is gone forever.

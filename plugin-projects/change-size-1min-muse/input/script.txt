Your alarm rings. You stretch. You grow... just enough to reach the top shelf. You shrink back down. Lily loses her earring. It rolls under the fridge. You shrink to ten centimeters. You walk underneath and find it. You hand it to her. She stares. You did it again... didn't you? You shrug. You grow back. Your mom calls from the kitchen. Noah. Breakfast. Now. And your tea... is still hot. Your name is Noah. You are sixteen. The average teenager is one meter seventy. You? Ten centimeters. Or ten meters. You can change your size. Any time. But there is a rule. Your body stays yours. Your muscles do not grow with you. Your bones do not get tougher. Grow ten times taller... and you are ten times weaker for your weight. Shrink ten times smaller... and a raindrop hits like a stone. So what if you could change your size?

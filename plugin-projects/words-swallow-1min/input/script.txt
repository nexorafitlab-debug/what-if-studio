Your mom slides the toast across the table. She smiles. Eat up... it's a good day. But above her head, grey words float. "I'M SORRY I MISSED YOUR GAME." You watch them fade. She never says them. She never will. What is she sorry for? She missed your game last Tuesday. She was working late. Six days she has carried it. And now it is gone. Your name is Eli. You are sixteen. And you can see the words people swallow. Every unsaid sentence floats above their head. Grey text. Soft. Like smoke. It hangs for a few seconds. Then it fades. Once a word fades, it is gone forever. You cannot make anyone say them. You can only watch... You can watch a whole life... disappear in grey letters. Once, just once, you saw a word turn gold. A girl told her friend she was sorry. The grey word turned gold and floated up, like light. So what if you could see the words people swallow?

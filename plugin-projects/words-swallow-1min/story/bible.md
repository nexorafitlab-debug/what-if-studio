# What If You Could See The Words People Swallow? — 1-min sample bible

## Hero
Eli, 16. Black round head, gold rim light, forest green hoodie #1E7B4D, dark pants,
white sneakers. Voice: Helio (avocado_v2:MAI_03).

## Swallowed-words device
Grey ALL-CAPS text (#b8b8c0, soft glow) floats just above a person's head, hangs,
then fades — once a word fades it is gone forever. Long lines split into two.
When someone finally SAYS a swallowed word out loud, the grey text turns GOLD
(#ffd76a) and rises like light (goldWord: grey from greyAt, gold from `at`, ~1 s).
Eli's OWN words are visible only in a mirror — never directly above his head.

## The rule (stated once, early)
You can see the words. You can NEVER make anyone say them. Once a word fades,
it is gone forever.
